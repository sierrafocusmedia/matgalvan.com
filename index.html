ABOUT MAT

Raised in the Sierra. Working for all of rural California.

Mat Galvan was raised in Tuolumne County and lives in Mi-Wuk Village. He chairs the Tuolumne County Democratic Central Committee, serves as Gold Country Regional Chair of the CDP Rural Caucus, and is a member of the Democratic State Central Committee.

He founded Sierra Focus Media, a strategy-first communications firm in Sonora, after 15 years in communications. His approach to party work is the same as his approach to client work: set clear goals, measure them, and report back. Receipts, not rhetoric.

POLICY PRIORITIES

What the caucus will carry to the party

The party already has a Rural Plank. These are the priorities the caucus will move through resolutions, legislation, and convenings, with one test for every policy the party supports: is it fair, and will it work in rural California?

Home insurance and wildfire

Keep coverage available and affordable in fire country, and back forest and land stewardship that works alongside the tribal nations who have cared for these lands since time immemorial.

Broadband

Push state broadband dollars all the way to the last mile, so rural families, schools, and small businesses can work and learn from home.

Rural health care

Protect rural hospitals and clinics, and make sure care is within reach without a two-hour drive.

Water

Clean, reliable water for the communities, farms, and ranches where it starts.

Workforce and local economies

Good jobs that let people build a life where they grew up, with labor, local business, and tribal partners at the table.

THE PLAN

Show Up. Build. Deliver.

Every goal is measurable, tracked on a scorecard, and reported to members.

01 SHOW UP

Bring the party to rural California in person, with a standing tour that brings statewide officials and party leaders into rural counties every year.

02 BUILD

Seat and activate every regional chair, grow caucus membership in every region, recruit Democrats for local office in every rural county, and give county parties a shared toolkit.

03 DELIVER

Move caucus resolutions every cycle on rural priorities, apply the Rural Plank test to the legislation the party supports, and bring the caucus through 2027 recertification with measurable goals on file.
