ABOUT MAT

Raised in the Sierra. Working for all of rural California.

Mat Galvan was raised in Tuolumne County and lives in Mi-Wuk Village. He chairs the Tuolumne County Democratic Central Committee, serves as Gold Country Regional Chair of the CDP Rural Caucus, and is a member of the Democratic State Central Committee.

He founded Sierra Focus Media, a strategy-first communications firm in Sonora, after 15 years in communications. His approach to party work is the same as his approach to client work: set clear goals, measure them, and report back. Receipts, not rhetoric.

THE PLAN

Show Up. Build. Deliver.

Every goal is measurable, tracked on a scorecard, and reported to members.

01 SHOW UP

Bring the party to rural California in person, with a standing tour that brings statewide officials and party leaders into rural counties every year.

02 BUILD

Seat and activate every regional chair, grow caucus membership in every region, recruit Democrats for local office in every rural county, and give county parties a shared toolkit.

03 DELIVER

Move caucus resolutions every cycle on rural priorities, apply the Rural Plank test to the legislation the party supports, and bring the caucus through 2027 recertification with measurable goals on file.

THE ACTION PLAN

What the caucus does, starting day one.

Not promises. A work plan the caucus can run inside the party, with results reported back to members.

FIRST 90 DAYS
  1. 01

    Seat every region

    Fill every open regional chair seat and hold the first full round of regional calls.

  2. 02

    Set the scorecard

    Adopt measurable caucus goals and publish them to members, so the 2027 recertification is ready long before it is due.

FIRST YEAR
  1. 03

    Run the resolutions pipeline

    Bring rural resolutions to every Executive Board and convention through the party's Resolutions and Legislation committees.

  2. 04

    Put the Rural Plank to work

    Ask the party to test the bills it backs with one question: is it fair, and will it work in rural California?

  3. 05

    Bring the party to rural counties

    Host statewide officials and party leaders in rural counties, region by region.

EVERY QUARTER
  1. 06

    Feed the bench

    Work with the party's local candidate recruitment so every rural county has Democrats on the ballot.

  2. 07

    Equip county parties

    Share bylaws templates, event playbooks, and volunteer systems so no small county party starts from scratch.

  3. 08

    Report back

    Send members a scorecard update in plain numbers. What moved, what did not, and what is next.
