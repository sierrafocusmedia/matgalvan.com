ABOUT MAT

Raised in the Sierra. Working for all of rural California.

Mat Galvan was raised in the Sierra Nevada and lives in Mi-Wuk Village in Tuolumne County with his partner. He is a small business owner, a past president of the Tuolumne County Chamber of Commerce, chair of the Tuolumne County Democratic Central Committee, and Gold Country Regional Chair of the CDP Rural Caucus.

He founded Sierra Focus Media in Sonora, a communications & Public Relations firm that has served more than 100 small businesses, economic development partners, and campaigns. His 15 years in communications include managing internal communications for companies like Amazon and Tribal Partners like the Chicken Ranch Rancheria of Me-Wuk Indians.

From 2024 to 2026 he was Industry Sector Coordinator for Recreation and Tourism with California Jobs First in the Sierra region, supporting partners behind $9 million in project investments. He brings the same approach to party work: set clear goals, measure them, and report back. Receipts, not rhetoric.

THE PLAN

Show Up. Build. Deliver.

Every goal is measurable, tracked on a scorecard, and reported to members.

01 SHOW UP

Bring the party to rural California in person, with a standing tour that brings statewide officials and party leaders into rural counties every year.

02 BUILD

Give every regional chair a defined role, grow caucus membership in every region, recruit Democrats for local office in every rural county, and give county parties a shared toolkit.

03 DELIVER

Move caucus resolutions every cycle on rural priorities, apply the Rural Plank test to the legislation the party supports, and bring the caucus through 2027 recertification with measurable goals on file.

CAUCUS GOALS

What the caucus does, starting day one.

Not promises. A work plan the caucus can run inside the party, with results reported back to members.

FIRST 90 DAYS
  1. 01

    Onboard the new team

    Hold an orientation for newly elected officers and regional chairs within 30 days, and give every regional chair one defined job, from membership to resolutions to events.

  2. 02

    Call every county party chair

    Reach each county party chair in the caucus and ask one question: what can the caucus do for your county?

  3. 03

    Set the scorecard

    Adopt measurable caucus goals and publish them to members, so the 2027 recertification is ready long before it is due.

FIRST YEAR
  1. 04

    Run the resolutions pipeline

    Bring rural resolutions to every Executive Board and convention through the party's Resolutions and Legislation committees.

  2. 05

    Put the Rural Plank to work

    Ask the party to test the bills it backs with one question: is it fair, and will it work in rural California?

  3. 06

    Bring the party to rural counties

    Host statewide officials and party leaders in rural counties, region by region.

EVERY QUARTER
  1. 07

    Feed the bench

    Work with the party's local candidate recruitment so every rural county has Democrats on the ballot.

  2. 08

    Equip county parties

    Share bylaws templates, event playbooks, and volunteer systems so no small county party starts from scratch.

  3. 09

    Report back

    Send members a scorecard update in plain numbers. What moved, what did not, and what is next.
